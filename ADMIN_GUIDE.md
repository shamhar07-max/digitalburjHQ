# Owner workflow

1. Start HQ and sign in to your owner account.
2. Open People → Invite staff. Set the job title and department. Share the single-use link privately.
3. After the person activates their account, create the specific job in Jobs & delivery and choose its assignee.
4. Open the job brief to find its record ID. In Access control, configure that user.
5. Choose Restricted job. Set the job's ID for View jobs, Update job progress and Discuss assigned jobs. Enable meetings, messages or other actions only if required. Save.
6. The person can see only the authorized job and actions. Assigning a role or department alone does not unlock records.
7. Give approval rights separately to the person who checks completion. Edit or remove permissions whenever responsibilities change.

## Example responsibilities

| Person | Practical assignment | Typical scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Academy teacher | Deliver one lesson and submit its review evidence | Specific job + own submitted reviews |
| Assessor | Review a particular assessment | Specific review + approval permission |
| Developer | Build one Business OS module | Assigned development jobs + discussion |
| Studio designer | Deliver one client brief | Specific Studio job + assigned resource |
| Growth coordinator | Train and support assigned partners | Assigned affiliate records; no payout approval |
| Finance reviewer | Review a selected partner's earnings | Specific affiliate ID for commission view/approval/payout |
| Department lead | Organize a team's delivery | Explicit department scopes, not inherited from title |

For commission permissions, the record scope is the affiliate's ID; it covers that affiliate's ledger entries. For messaging record scopes, use the permitted contact's staff ID. Access changes are checked on every backend request.

## Communication

Messages lists permitted contacts and personal threads. Opening a conversation marks received messages read. Unread counts appear in navigation and the top bar. Notifications show personal action updates. Both refresh every 15 seconds when not editing.

Schedule meetings with the agenda, dates, participants and genuine Google Meet link. Participants need View meetings with assigned or specific meeting scope. To automatically generate links, configure the optional authorized Google adapter described in README.

## Affiliation workflow

Register partner → record training → activate → record verified customer payment with an evidence reference → wait for refund hold → approve commission → confirm Founding 100 qualification when eligible → record a completed external payout reference.

No partner recruitment payments are created. Commission comes from eligible recorded customer sales. Keep public partner terms aligned with the configured rates and deadlines.

## Boundaries

This is the internal operations application. Live learners, customers, checkout, payment verification, automatic transfers and external affiliate accounts need separate integrations. No sample records appear in the live database.
